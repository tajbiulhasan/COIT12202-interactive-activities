## Interactive Activities

The repository contains interactive activities completed from Week 5 to Week 10.


### Week 5 – Kerberos Authentication

The Week 5 activities focus on Kerberos authentication and security concepts.

Topics include:

* Kerberos concepts and message flow
* AS-REQ and AS-REP
* TGS-REQ and TGS-REP
* AP-REQ and AP-REP
* Ticket Granting Ticket (TGT)
* Service Tickets
* Pass-the-Ticket attacks
* Golden Ticket attacks
* Silver Ticket attacks
* Kerberoasting
* Kerberos security mitigations


### Week 6 – Firewalls and Security Zones

The Week 6 activities focus on firewall operation and security policies.

Topics include:

* Stateful firewall operation
* TCP connection tracking
* Firewall state tables
* Firewall rule troubleshooting
* Security zones
* Zone-based firewall policies
* Traffic inspection
* Firewall rule analysis
* Outbound traffic rules


### Week 7 – VPN and IPsec

The Week 7 activities focus on VPN technologies and secure communication.

Topics include:

* VPN fundamentals
* Encryption
* Tunnelling
* Remote-access VPN
* VPN authentication methods
* VPN security considerations
* IPsec
* IKEv2
* IKE_SA_INIT
* IKE_AUTH
* CREATE_CHILD_SA
* ESP traffic


### Week 8 – IDS, Snort and Risk Management

The Week 8 activities focus on intrusion detection and cybersecurity risk management.

Topics include:

* IDS and IPS fundamentals
* Signature-based detection
* Detection outcomes
* True Positive
* False Positive
* False Negative
* True Negative
* IDS alert tuning
* Snort rule components
* Risk identification
* Risk analysis
* Risk evaluation
* Risk treatment
* Residual risk


### Week 9 – Wireless Networking

The Week 9 activities focus on wireless networking concepts.

Topics include:

* Wireless Access Points
* Infrastructure mode
* Wireless network terminology
* Wi-Fi standards
* 802.11b
* 802.11g
* 802.11n
* 802.11ac
* 802.11ax
* 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz wireless bands


### Week 10 – Cloud Security

The Week 10 activities focus on cloud computing and cloud security.

Topics include:

* On-Premises
* Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)
* Platform as a Service (PaaS)
* Software as a Service (SaaS)
* Shared responsibility model
* Cloud security threats
* Account hijacking
* Misconfiguration
* Identity and Access Management (IAM)
* Principle of least privilege
* Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
* Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
* Data at rest
* Data in transit
* Data in use
